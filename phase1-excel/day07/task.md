# Day 07 — Executive Dashboard & KPI Report

## Goal
Combine everything from Days 01–06 into a single-page executive dashboard.

## Tasks
1. On a new sheet `Dashboard`, build a single-page view with:
   - 4 KPI cards (cell blocks) at the top: Total Revenue, Total Orders,
     Average Order Value, Total Quantity Sold
   - A bar chart: Revenue by Region
   - A line chart: Revenue trend by Month (you'll need a helper column
     extracting Month from OrderDate)
   - A table: Top 5 Products by Revenue
   - A slicer for `Region` or `Channel` that filters the charts
2. Format for presentation: consistent color palette, no gridlines showing,
   clear titles on every chart.
3. Save as `day07_solved.xlsx` — this is your **Phase 1 capstone**, a
   dashboard you could screenshot for a portfolio or LinkedIn post.

## Deliverable
`day07_solved.xlsx` — one polished `Dashboard` sheet.
