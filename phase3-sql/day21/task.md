# Day 21 — Business Insights: Top Products & Peak-Time Trends

## Goal
Answer real business questions using everything from Days 15–20 combined.

## Tasks
Write queries (with comments explaining the business question each answers):
1. Top 5 products by total revenue, and their % share of total revenue.
2. Which day of week generates the most average revenue per order?
   (use a date function to extract weekday)
3. Which Region + Category combination is most profitable, assuming a
   30% margin on Electronics/Furniture and 15% on Grocery/Apparel/Stationery?
4. Identify "at risk" sales reps: reps whose most recent order month has
   lower revenue than their average monthly revenue.
5. A single summary query combining rank + aggregation: Top 3 SalesReps
   per Region by revenue (window function + filter).

## Deliverable
`day21_solved.sql` — this is your **Phase 3 capstone**, a set of 5
business-question queries ready to present.
